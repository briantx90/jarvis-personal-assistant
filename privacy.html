Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Jarvis Personal Assistant, a personal-use application.

Access and use

With the account holder's authorization, the application accesses Google email, calendar, file, document, and spreadsheet data, including content and metadata, to fulfill requested tasks. Authorized tasks may include sending communications or updating and sharing data.

Processing and disclosure

Relevant data may be processed by hosting and AI service providers to provide requested assistance, subject to their applicable policies and account settings. The application operator does not sell account data or use it for advertising. Disclosure to other recipients occurs as authorized by the account holder or as required by law.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored to maintain authorized access. Task data may remain in conversation records, logs, or generated files until deleted or removed under applicable retention settings. Provider-side retention may differ. OAuth, HTTPS for Google API requests, and restricted credential-file access are used; absolute security is not guaranteed.

Control and deletion

Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Revocation does not automatically delete previously stored records. The account holder can separately delete application records and use relevant provider deletion controls.

Google API policy

Use and transfer of Google API data must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Website and contact

This informational website contains no account data, sign-in forms, or analytics scripts. Its hosting provider may process standard visitor connection information. Questions may be directed to the support email shown on the application's Google consent screen.
